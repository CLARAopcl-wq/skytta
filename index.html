Origin

From the Field
to the Future

Started in the trades — concrete, excavation, the work that builds the world. Learned early that the biggest problems aren't on the job site, they're in the office. Missed leads. Lost invoices. Crews waiting on information that never came.

Instead of complaining about broken tools, built new ones. Blueprint Hub Operations was born from real frustration on real job sites — a CRM built by a contractor, for contractors.

That turned into a web design business (DCSites), then an AI app platform (Appytta), then a full automation system that qualifies leads and texts them back in seconds — automatically.

The limit of possibility keeps moving. That's the point.
